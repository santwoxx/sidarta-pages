Scroll

"Not seduction — ritual. A single bottle, a single tradition, held before something that matters."

Sultan Al-Oud is built around anointing — the real Arabian custom of rubbing oud oil between the palms before prayer or a special occasion. The bottle carries that weight: a facetted crystal flacon, an onyx cap, a gold seal engraved with the letter س — Sin, for Sultan. A single tradition, held before something that matters.
