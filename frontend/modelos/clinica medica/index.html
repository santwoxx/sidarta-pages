Technology

Precision, Engineered

High-Field MRI

Detailed soft-tissue and disc imaging without radiation — the gold standard for diagnosing spine, joint and ligament pathology.

Digital X-ray / EOS

Low-dose full-body weight-bearing imaging of the spine and joints — providing the standing posture data critical for surgical planning.

Surgical Robotics

Mako robotic-arm navigation for millimeter-precise joint replacement — patient-specific planning executed with sub-millimeter accuracy intraoperatively.

Shockwave Unit

Focused acoustic therapy for chronic tendon and bone conditions — non-invasive, no anaesthesia, proven results for calcific tendinitis and plantar fasciitis.

Motion Lab

Sensor-based gait and posture analysis — revealing how you load your joints and where movement deviations cause pain, forming the blueprint for targeted treatment.

Intraoperative Navigation

Real-time GPS-precision tracking during surgery — our navigation system maps bone anatomy in 3D, guiding implant placement to sub-millimetre accuracy for optimal alignment and longevity.

Your Journey

From First Visit to Full Movement

01

Consultation

We listen first. Full medical history, physical examination and a frank discussion of your goals — before any imaging or planning begins.

02

Imaging & Diagnosis

MRI, X-ray or EOS imaging delivers a precise diagnosis you can see and understand. No guesswork — only evidence-based findings.

03

Treatment

Conservative or surgical — always tailored to you. We choose the least invasive path that achieves the best outcome for your body and lifestyle.

04

Recovery

Physiotherapy, follow-up imaging and lifetime check-ins. We stay with you until you move freely — and beyond.
